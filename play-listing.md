# Play Store listing

Title (30 characters max): Unto Godliness

Short description (80 characters max):
A nightly self-examination. Train yourself for godliness. Nothing leaves your phone.

Full description:

Every evening, at the hour you choose, Unto Godliness asks you a short list of
questions about your day, one at a time. Did you pray? Did you read? Did you serve
someone? Did you forgive? You answer yes or no, add a number or a note where it
helps, and the app shows you the result of the day and how you are doing over time.

The questions are yours. Edit them, group them in sections, ask some only on certain
days or on certain days of the month, add a follow-up question that appears only when
you answer no. Start from the built-in list or from an empty page.

What you get
- A daily reminder at the time you choose, and a second one later if you have not answered
- A morning question to set an intention for the day
- Statistics: what you do most faithfully, where you struggle, streaks per question
- A weekly review every Sunday
- A home screen widget with the score of the day
- A printable monthly sheet, questions in rows and days in columns, as a PDF
- Automatic backup to a file on your device, import and export

What you do not get
- No account, no sign-in
- No ads
- No analytics, no tracking
- No internet connection at all. Nothing leaves your phone.

"Train yourself for godliness; for while bodily training is of some value, godliness
is of value in every way, as it holds promise for the present life and also for the
life to come." 1 Timothy 4:7-8

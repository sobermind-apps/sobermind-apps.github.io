Privacy Policy

Unto Godliness, published by Sober Mind Apps.
Last updated: September 8, 2026.

The short version

Unto Godliness does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app makes no network requests.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores your questions, your daily answers, notes and settings in the app's private storage on your device. This data is never sent anywhere. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or when you use the Reset function in Settings.

If you use the Backup functions, the app writes a copy of your data to a file in the Documents folder of your device, or produces a file you download. That file is created only when you ask for it, and it stays under your control. If you choose to share it through another app, that other app's privacy policy applies to the copy you shared.

Permissions

Notifications: used only to show your daily reminder at the time you choose. You can refuse this permission; the app still works without it.

Storage (older Android versions only): used only to write the backup file when you ask for it.

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or any advertising identifier.

Accounts, analytics, advertising

There is no account to create. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third party SDK that communicates over the network.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address, with the date updated above.
